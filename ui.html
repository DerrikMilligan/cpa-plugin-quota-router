Use a usage-limit reset?

This spends the reset with Anthropic and cannot be undone.

Headroom is weekly room times reset urgency, plus the 5-hour room scaled by five-hour-weight. A window's room is its ceiling minus its usage; urgency grows as the weekly reset nears, so weekly room that is about to expire is used first. New conversations go to the account with the most headroom. Both ceilings are hard limits. Hover a headroom number to see its calculation. The dark tick on each bar is the ceiling. Blank ceiling inputs use the defaults. Saving updates that account in the plugin config and applies at once.
